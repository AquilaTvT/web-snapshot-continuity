\title{Uniqueness Is Not Continuity: Supplementary Material}
\subtitle{Complete proofs, protocols and reproduction}
\begin{abstract}
This document holds the complete statements and proofs behind the four-page
paper \emph{Uniqueness Is Not Continuity: When Server Metadata Certifies a Web
Snapshot}, the protocols and full results of its executions and replay, and
the commands that reproduce every number. Results keep the paper's numbering.
Section~\ref{sec:map} maps each claim of the paper to its proof, data and code.
Every table of counts is generated from the derived evidence by
\code{scripts/supplement_tables.py}, and every number shared with the paper
comes from the same generated macros. The paper does not depend on this
document.
\end{abstract}
