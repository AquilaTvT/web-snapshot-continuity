Delete: delete the newest version by ID. Marker: insert and remove a delete marker. Lock: the same over a COMPLIANCE-retained version. Susp.: write A, B, A with versioning suspended. Copy, Upload: restore A by copying its version or uploading its bytes. $\bullet$ an identifier read before the change is current again after it, so equal identifiers would certify a false continuity; $\circ$ the identifier differs, or no version is current; $\times$ the request failed; -- no such operation. Every S3 upload repeats the first ETag.
